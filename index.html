How to play

Pick housemates within your 100 credits (bigger names cost more), confirm your squad, then choose your starting players and a captain in My team. After each episode your players earn or lose points for what happens in the house.

Squad, starters and bench

You pick up to 15 housemates. 11 start and score points; the rest sit on the bench and score nothing. Nothing swaps automatically, you decide. As housemates get evicted your squad shrinks to match: the bench goes first, then the starters, down to a minimum of 5.

Captain

Your captain must be one of your starters. Their points count double.

Evictions

If one of your housemates is evicted they leave your squad. Tap Edit squad on the Draft tab to pick a replacement.

Deadline

Each gameweek has a deadline shown at the top of the Draft tab. After it passes, your team is locked until the next one.

Fair play

Free to play, no money or prizes. An unofficial fan game, not affiliated with Banijay, Asianet or JioHotstar. Points are added by hand after each episode, so they can take a little while to show up.
